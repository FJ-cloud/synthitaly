The pattern to read across every study: the naive column includes the LEAK_ debt-mechanic features and is the control condition — it measures how completely the label is written into the ledger by construction. The fair column is the honest result: what ordinary, bank-observable behaviour actually carries.

Studies 0, A and B ask about debtors; studies C and D ask the same questions of savers. Reading them together is what makes each one mean something — the two labels behave oppositely, and the contrast is the methodological result.

“Fair” is relative to the label you are predicting. The LEAK_ prefix quarantines what mechanically encodes debtor status. Two columns that are genuinely fair for that label — cur_total_out and cur_balance — encode saver status via the month-close sweep, so the saver studies use a separate LEAK_SAVER quarantine. The debtor feature set is left exactly as it was, so every debtor number on this page is unchanged. See Study D.
study 0

Is the feature matrix factorable?

PCA and KMeans both assume the correlation matrix carries common structure worth extracting. This tests that assumption instead of taking it on faith. The first row is the point: the untouched fair set is singular — the ten share_* columns are proportions of one total and sum to exactly 1.0 for every consumer — and diagnostics.kmo refuses to pseudo-invert it rather than returning a plausible number computed from noise.

Bartlett rejects sphericity in every case (p ≈ 0), so structure certainly exists. The middling KMO says that structure is only partly common-factor shaped — which is what you expect from a generator whose latent labels are drawn rather than caused. study A

Clustering — do the archetypes fall out of the ledger?

KMeans (k = 3) on the debtor subpopulation, scored against the true climber / chronic / subsister labels.

Subsisters separate cleanly because they draw on a credit line — a distinct mechanic that leaves a distinct trace. Climbers and chronics differ only in repayment speed, so clustering merges them; that is the ceiling, and it is structural rather than a tuning failure.

The figures quote ARI 0.39 / 0.01 rather than the 0.471 / 0.200 above — not a contradiction. Figures are generated at 600 consumers × 720 days on the small FAIR_COMPACT / LEAK_COMPACT column set, where the point is a stable pinned number; the tables here use the full 45-column frame at 800 × 720. Both are correct for their configuration, and the configuration is stated wherever a number appears.

study B

Prediction — can debtors and climbers be spotted?

5-fold cross-validated ROC-AUC. Two tasks, two estimators, each measured on the leaked control set and on fair features only.

is_debtor is near-perfect with the debt-mechanic columns and modest without them. The label is drawn by numbers.has_debt, a Bernoulli on the income-quartile gradient {1: 0.120, 2: 0.192, 3: 0.244, 4: 0.285}, so at assignment time income quartile is the only signal in it and bounds the Bayes-optimal AUC at 0.603. The fair scores clear that bound on post-assignment repayment behaviour. is_climber predicts better fair-only because the divergent repayment rules leave an ordinary, visible trace in the account record.

One leak hid inside the fair set. The fair debtor AUC read 0.91 until the feature set was audited: cur_n_entries, a raw count of current-account entries, silently included the debt-service, credit-draw and overdraft lines. It is now LEAK_cur_n_entries and the honest number is 0.697. A feature is not fair because its name is innocuous — only if the generating process cannot write the label into it. Full audit in docs/EXPLANATION.md §8a.
study C

Clustering — saver vs non-saver

The same question as Study A, asked of the other label. KMeans at k = 2 against is_saver, and at k = 4 against the four-way financial_status (saver / non-saver, each with and without debt).

FeaturesnkagainstARINMIsilhouette
naive442is_saver0.02220.01300.2996

saver-fair322is_saver0.01800.01070.3620
saver-fair324financial_status0.03230.02740.1493
Clustering does not find savers — and that is the result. ARI ≈ 0.008 at k = 2. Look at the naive row: even with the label mechanically present in the features — where prediction below scores 0.9999 — clustering still cannot recover it. Saver status is a real but low-variance direction in the feature space; KMeans partitions on the dominant axes (income scale, activity volume) and this split is not one of them. The healthy silhouette (0.38) confirms it found a clean structure, just not this one.

Put beside Study A — where the debtor archetypes reached ARI 0.471 — the pair makes the methodological point neither makes alone: clustering recovers a label only when it aligns with a dominant axis of variation; prediction only needs the signal to be present at all.

study D

Prediction — who is a saver?

5-fold cross-validated ROC-AUC on is_saver, under three feature sets. The middle column is the one to read.

is_saver800 (442)logreg1.00000.99170.7467
is_saver800 (442)rf0.99990.99170.7811
A second leak, on a different label. The debtor-fair column is the set this repo calls "fair" — and on the saver label it is not fair at all, scoring 0.995. Consumer._month_close sweeps the month's positive residual into savings or pension, and that sweep is a debit on the current account. So cur_total_out counts a line only savers ever have, and cur_balance is what is left after it. Quarantining the two as LEAK_SAVER takes the honest number to 0.827. Same class of bug as cur_n_entries, found by asking the question of a new label.

The audit — correlation with is_saver

Correlating with the label is not what makes a column unfair; mechanically encoding it is. Three columns correlate as strongly and are kept, each for its own reason:

Columncorrdisposition
cur_total_out+0.612quarantined — the sweep debit lands in it
cur_balance-0.235quarantined — the sweep debit lands in it
balance_std_proxy+0.351kept — transaction-derived; sweeps are never written to model.transactions
cur_total_in+0.294kept — account-derived but unreachable: the sweep credits savings/pension, never the current account
mean_income_credit+0.292kept — salary credits only — genuine signal, and income quintile is the label's actual cause

LEAK_savings_balance is zero for 100% of non-savers — definitionally the label, and already quarantined.

Why this label is easier, and one confound

Honest saver prediction (0.827) beats honest debtor prediction (0.697), and the reason is structural: is_saver is drawn on income_quintile, which the ledger does show through the income credits. The debtor subtype is drawn on a hidden binary flag. The label has an observable cause, so behaviour carries it.

Known confound: subsisters are force-set is_saver = True in ItalyModel._assign_savings, so the two labels are entangled — chronic: 26/44 savers, climber: 50/86 savers, none: 329/633 savers, subsister: 37/37 savers.

Where everything comes from

Two tables, kept apart on purpose. The first is where the model's numbers come from — empirical, Italian, load-bearing for realism. The second is where the analysis methods come from — canonical statistics, load-bearing for defensibility. A method citation is never evidence that a magnitude is real.
